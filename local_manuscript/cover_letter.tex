\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled \textbf{"Cycle-Aware Forman-Ricci Curvature for Signed and Bipartite Networks,"} which we are submitting for exclusive consideration of publication as a research article in \textit{SIAM Undergraduate Research Online (SIURO)}.

\textbf{About the Paper and Its Importance:}
In this manuscript, we present a novel Cycle-Aware mathematical framework that extends standard Forman-Ricci Curvature to effectively analyze bipartite and signed networks without succumbing to metric collapse. By utilizing structurally bounded 4-cycle paths instead of absent 3-cycles, we restore the ability to detect and quantify dense mesoscopic clustering within these complex graphs. We also developed an optimized Masked Sparse-Sparse Matrix Multiplication (SpSpMM) algorithm that computes this metric deterministically with $\mathcal{O}(\sum d(v) d_{max})$ bounded time complexity. When validated on massive datasets like MovieLens-1M and Amazon Product Reviews, our zero-parameter geometric metric achieves predictive accuracy comparable to state-of-the-art Graph Neural Networks (like LightGCN) at a fraction of the computational cost.

\textbf{Originality Statement:}
We confirm that this manuscript represents original research. It has not been previously published in any peer-reviewed venue, nor is it currently under consideration for publication by any other journal.
